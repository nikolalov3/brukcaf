BRUK CAFE. Specialty coffee in Kleparz, Kraków

BRUK CAFE is a specialty coffee shop in Kleparz, Kraków, at Krótka 1, a few minutes' walk from Kraków Main Station and the Old Town. We serve specialty coffee, espresso and filter methods such as V60, matcha, breakfast and simple food. We have Wi-Fi and power outlets at the tables.

Coffee near Kraków Main Station

BRUK CAFE is about a 5-minute walk from Kraków Główny and Galeria Krakowska. A good stop for specialty coffee and breakfast before you head into the city.

Specialty coffee in Kleparz

BRUK is in the Kleparz district, just behind the Kraków Barbican and close to the Old Town. We focus on specialty coffee, including espresso and filter methods such as V60.

Near the Old Town, away from the crowds

BRUK sits just outside the busiest part of the Old Town, so it's an easy place for coffee, breakfast or a short break while you explore central Kraków.

Working from a café in Kraków

BRUK has Wi-Fi and power outlets at the tables, so it's also a good spot for working with a laptop over coffee.
