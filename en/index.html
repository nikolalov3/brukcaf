02Inside

The best coffee in Kleparz, a few steps from the Old Town.

A raw space just north of the Planty park. Close to everything, already out of the tourist crush of the Main Square. Concrete, quiet and enough room to stay a while: to work, to talk, to take a video call.

For espresso we grind HAYB Yellow, a dark blend of one hundred percent arabica from Brazil and Guatemala. The rest of the menu is V60. Plus matcha, cold brew and breakfast until one.

05Questions

Can I work here with a laptop?

You can. Wi-Fi, power sockets by the tables and quiet enough for a meeting or a video call.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are welcome.

Is there a garden or outdoor seating?

No. All of Bruk is inside: a raw concrete space made for staying a while.

What coffee do you brew?

For espresso we grind HAYB Yellow, a dark blend of one hundred percent arabica from Brazil and Guatemala. The rest of the menu is V60, and we keep more beans around, so just ask the staff.

What milk options do you have?

Coconut, almond, oat, dairy and lactose free.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Plant based options are marked in the menu with veggie and vegan tags.

Until what time do you serve breakfast?

Every day from 9:00 to 13:00, weekends included.

How do I get there from Kraków Main Station?

A five minute walk, right behind the Barbican. Krótka 1 in Kleparz.
