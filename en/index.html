02Inside

The best coffee in Kleparz, a few steps from the Old Town.

We sit just north of the Planty park, close to everything, but already out of the tourist crush of the Main Square. We brew specialty coffee: doppio, flat white, V60 pour-over. Plus matcha in several forms, cold brew and breakfast until one. Raw interior, warm service, and a power socket by your table if you came here to work.
